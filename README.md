## ONLINE RETAIL SALES ANLAYSIS
This project analyzes a 3 year online retail company sales data to identify trends, customer behaviour, coupon usage, product performance,
and revenue.

## OBJECTIVES
- Analyze sales trend
- Identify top selling product
- Identify top performing referral source
- Evaluate coupon effectiveness.

  ## TOOL USED
  EXCEL
  (DAX MEASURE)
  (PIVOT TABLE)
  SQL
  POWER BI (VISUALIZATION)

  ## ANALYSIS PROCESS
  I check for duplicate and null values, found no duplicate, but there are null values in coupon code column,
  which was later filled up because of the kind of data it is.
  Summary statistic was done on the dataset.
  Visualization was then done using power BI
  
  ## KEY INSIGHTS
  - Chair is the top selling product which also doubles as product that generate highest revenue.
  - Instagram is the top referral source.
  - In 2024 and 2025 FREESHIP drives the most revenue , followed by No coupon  .
  - Customers with "NO Coupon" contributed to more sales.
  - Yearly,there is continous sales decline and about 100% decline in 2025,because there is only 6month sales data provided that year .
  - Quantity sold is 3535 while items in cart is 6582,total unpurchased good stood at 3047.

    ## RECOMMENDATION
    I would like to recommend that Instagram marketing should be increased because it top the chart in sales volume and revenue.
    The company should investigate the cause of unpurchased items in cart.
    The company should also look into reason for yearly sales decline.
    Chairs stocking should have the highest priority, followed closely by laptop.
