#Decodelabs-Internship

## ONLINE RETAIL SALES ANLAYSIS
This project analyzes an online retail company sales data to identify trends, customer behaviour, coupon usage, product performance,
and revenue.

## OBJECTIVES
- Analyze sales trend
- Identify top selling product
- Identify top performing referral source
- Evaluate coupon effectiveness.

  ## TOOL USED
  EXCEL
  (DAX MEASURE)
  (PIVOT TABLE)
  ## KEY INSIGHTS
  - Chair is the top selling product which also doubles as product that generate highest revenue.
  - Instagram is the top referral source.
  - FREESHIP is the most used Coupon.
  - Customers with "NO Coupon" contributed to more sales.
  - Yearly continously there is sales decline and about 100% decline in 2025.
  - Quantity sold is not equal to items in cart.
